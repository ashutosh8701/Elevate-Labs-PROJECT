Startup Investment Analysis — Shark Tank India
Overview
Analysis of startup investment trends using the Shark Tank India dataset — 634 pitches across 4 seasons and 80 fields. The analysis covers funding patterns, deal conversion, valuation gaps, investor activity and industry-wise investment trends using Excel, Python and Tableau.
Key Findings
* 634 pitches analysed across 4 seasons; the raw dataset was 54.5% sparse (27,647 missing values) before cleaning
* 66.7% of pitches received an offer; 85.1% of those offers converted, giving a 56.8% overall deal-conversion rate
* ₹262.9 Cr of total capital deployed across 360 funded deals
* Median deal closed at ₹60L against a median ask of ₹70L
* Median valuation haircut of 50% — founders asked ₹32 Cr and closed at ₹12.5 Cr
* Founders gave up 5% median equity against the 2.5% originally offered — double the intended dilution
* Beauty/Fashion (₹59.4 Cr) and Food & Beverage (₹53.7 Cr) attracted the most capital
* Medical/Health had the highest conversion rate at 73.2%, despite ranking fourth by pitch volume
* Most active investors: Aman (₹54.5 Cr, 141 deals), Peyush (₹40.7 Cr, 104 deals), Namita (₹40.6 Cr, 114 deals)
* Deals averaged 1.96 investors, indicating syndication was the norm rather than the exception
