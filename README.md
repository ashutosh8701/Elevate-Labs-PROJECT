# Startup Investment Analysis — Shark Tank India

## 📌 Overview

Analysis of startup investment trends using the **Shark Tank India dataset** — 634 pitches across 4 seasons and 80 fields. The analysis covers funding patterns, deal conversion, valuation gaps, investor activity and industry-wise investment trends using **Excel, Python and Tableau**.

## 🔍 Key Findings

- Analysed **634 pitches across 4 seasons**; the raw dataset was **54.5% sparse**, containing **27,647 missing values** before cleaning.
- **66.7% of pitches received an offer**, and **85.1% of those offers converted**, resulting in a **56.8% overall deal-conversion rate**.
- **₹262.9 Cr** of total capital was deployed across **360 funded deals**.
- Median deal closed at **₹60L** against a median founder ask of **₹70L**.
- Median valuation haircut was **50%** — founders asked **₹32 Cr** and closed at **₹12.5 Cr**.
- Founders gave up **5% median equity** against the **2.5% originally offered**, representing double the intended dilution.
- **Beauty/Fashion (₹59.4 Cr)** and **Food & Beverage (₹53.7 Cr)** attracted the most capital.
- **Medical/Health** recorded the highest conversion rate at **73.2%**, despite ranking fourth by pitch volume.
- Most active investors:
  - **Aman — ₹54.5 Cr across 141 deals**
  - **Peyush — ₹40.7 Cr across 104 deals**
  - **Namita — ₹40.6 Cr across 114 deals**
- Deals averaged **1.96 investors**, indicating that syndication was the norm rather than the exception.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Clean and preprocess a heavily sparse startup investment dataset.
- Quantify deal conversion and the gap between founder ask and closed terms.
- Analyse funding concentration by industry.
- Profile investor activity and syndication behaviour.
- Visualise investor and startup insights using **Tableau**.
